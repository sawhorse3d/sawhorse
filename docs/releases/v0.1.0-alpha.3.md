# Sawhorse 0.1.0-alpha.3

A pre-release: free, open source (MIT OR Apache-2.0), no account. Try it in the browser at https://sawhorse3d.com/app/ or install it below.

## What is new since 0.1.0-alpha.2

**Section planes you can trust**
- **The cap closes the half it caps, whatever the geometry.** The section cut is rebuilt on the solid's own triangles: every corner's distance from the plane is worked out once, the faces the plane splits are clipped along the triangles the solid's volume is measured by, and the cap is laid from exactly the edges that clipping leaves open. The alpha.2 caveat (in about one of every 130,000 random edit sequences, a face nudged to within a micrometre of a plane and then drawn on left the cut-away half without a cap) is gone, and so is the family of cases behind it: the property tests and the fuzzer now hold every cut to the cross-section of the solid worked out without the cut, at 100,000 cases a run (ADR-0010, lane 19c).
- **Faster.** A cut through the 13,562-face office fit-out takes 0.3 to 1.8 ms instead of 54 to 57 ms, with the same caps.
- A face lying exactly on a plane counts as kept, so the two ways a plane can face through it give different caps by design ([user guide](https://sawhorse3d.com/guide/#section-planes)).

**Getting around the model**
- **Orbit turns about the point under the cursor, and that point stays put** ([#5](https://github.com/sawhorse3d/sawhorse/issues/5)). Pressing the middle button picks the pivot (a corner or an edge near the cursor, else the face or the ground under it), a small crosshair marks it while you drag, and the model turns about it without swinging away. No lock near straight down or up: the tilt stops half a degree short and the turn across keeps working. Right-drag and Shift+middle-drag pan with the point under the cursor; the wheel zooms toward the cursor.
- **View > Grid** shows and hides the ground grid, as does **Ground grid** in the Styles pane ([#4](https://github.com/sawhorse3d/sawhorse/issues/4)). It is one undo step and is saved with the file and with each scene. Points on the ground still snap to grid steps when it is hidden.

**For the shop**
- **A whole piece of furniture drawn as one block is not a board.** A coffee table drawn as one closed 16 x 24 x 48 in block counted 128 board feet of 4/4. A part thicker than any board and over 12 in both ways, a thick solid more than 30% empty (a lamp, a stool) and lumber wider than 4 ft are now listed with their reason and not counted or nested; the Cut list's header says how many and why.
- **No ellipsis in the Cut list table.** Names, sizes and notes wrap instead of being cut off ("22 13/..."), the reason a part is not counted shows under its row instead of in a tooltip, and the panel is wider.

**Fixed**
- No triangle of a face stands off it: a face whose corners lie nearly in a line along an edge no longer gets a sliver triangle folded over a neighbouring face (it showed up in section caps; the volume, exports and booleans use the same triangles).
- Section cap rings never cross, including a hole touching its outer ring and caps clipped by a second plane.
- The render service's health check takes only the service's own answer: a catch-all server (a static host, a router's page, a sign-in page) no longer shows the Credits chip and the Render pane.
- The docs say only what is true: `1'-1-1/2"` reads as 13 1/2 in, the SketchUp 2018 issue is a draft not yet filed, and OpenCutList is free to download, open source (GPLv3) and donor-funded.

The full list is in the [changelog](https://sawhorse3d.com/changelog/).

## Not yet

- `.skp` export (you can open SketchUp files, not save them).
- Fillet and chamfer tools.
- Copy and paste in place.
- Back and Bottom views.
- Text, and radius or diameter dimensions in the model.
- Signed installers (see below; the plan is in the [code signing policy](../SIGNING.md)).
- Render credits: not launched; the app shows nothing about them unless you set a render service of your own in Edit > Settings.

## Known limits

- **Sketch geometry cannot be moved or rotated before it is pushed into a solid** ([#2](https://github.com/sawhorse3d/sawhorse/issues/2)): Move and Rotate refuse a loose circle, rectangle or line. Push/pull it first, or redraw it where you want it.
- **A circle, arc or polygon is selected one segment at a time** ([#3](https://github.com/sawhorse3d/sawhorse/issues/3)): clicking it picks the segment or corner under the cursor, not the whole curve.
- **A scene saved with the default style does not bring the grid back.** Such a scene leaves the style as it is when you go to it, so a grid hidden after the scene was saved stays hidden. Show it again with View > Grid, or update the scene while the grid shows.
- Models of a few thousand faces are quick; a 13.5k-face SketchUp office opened in about 4.5 s on the development machine. [docs/LIMITS.md](../LIMITS.md) has the measurements and the hard limits.
- openskp 1.3.0 refuses a real SketchUp 2018 save and files from SketchUp 2012 and older ([what comes across from the rest](../skp-compat.md)).
- Parts sized from their bounds are exact for boards and generous for anything slanted or rounded: check them against what you mean to build.
- The installers are not code-signed yet (below).

## Unsigned installers

The installers are built in the open by GitHub Actions but are **not code-signed yet**, so the system warns before the first launch. To open them:

**Windows** (the `.exe` or the `.msi`)
1. Run the installer. Windows shows "Windows protected your PC".
2. Click **More info**.
3. Click **Run anyway**.

**macOS** (the `.dmg`, Apple Silicon)
1. Open the disk image and drag Sawhorse to Applications.
2. Open Sawhorse. macOS says it cannot verify the app; click **Done** (not Move to Trash).
3. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll to **Security**: "Sawhorse was blocked to protect your Mac". Click **Open Anyway**.
4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click **Open Anyway** once more. Later launches open normally.

**Linux**: the `.AppImage` needs to be made executable (`chmod +x Sawhorse_*.AppImage`); the `.deb` and `.rpm` install as usual.

To check a download against the release, put it next to `SHA256SUMS.txt` and run `sha256sum -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS.txt` (Linux; on macOS `shasum -a 256 -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS.txt`; on Windows compare `Get-FileHash <file>` with its line).
