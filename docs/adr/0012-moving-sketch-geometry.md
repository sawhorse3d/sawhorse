# ADR-0012: Moving sketch geometry

- Status: accepted (lane 22, 2026-10-19)
- Issue: [#2](https://github.com/sawhorse3d/sawhorse/issues/2), "Move and Rotate refuse sketch geometry"

## Context

Until lane 22 the Move, Rotate and Scale tools acted on solids, groups, components, section planes and parts of one solid only. A circle, rectangle, polygon, arc or line that had not been pushed into a solid lives in a sketch (ADR-0001: a planar graph of vertices and edges on one plane, with regions re-detected after every edit), and the tools refused it: "Can't do that. Sketch edges don't move yet." A SketchUp user moves a face or a loose rectangle in the second minute; a SketchUcation regular reported the refusal on the first day of alpha.2.

What SketchUp does, as a user knows it: a selected face, edge or point moves with Move and Rotate exactly like a solid; moving one corner of a face drags the edges at it; a face moved onto other geometry merges with it; Ctrl copies. A face stays flat: a move that would bend it out of its plane is not allowed to.

## Decision

One kernel op, `TransformSketch { sketch, points, edges, regions, xform, copy, count }` (`crates/kernel/src/sketch_move.rs`), one per sketch a gesture touches; a selection that mixes solids and sketch geometry is one `Batch` (one undo step). The selection names sketch vertices by world position, edges by their two ends, and whole regions by id. The moving vertices are the points, the edges' ends and the regions' ring corners; the carried edges are the selected edges and the regions' ring edges.

- **Within the plane** (the map keeps the sketch's plane: a move along it, a turn about its normal, a flat scale), and **for part of a shape whatever the map** (a point, an edge: the edges beside it have to follow, and they must stay in the plane), every moving vertex goes to its mapped position projected into the plane. Every edge touching a moving vertex is taken out and drawn again between its ends' new positions with the sketch's own insertion rules (`Sketch::with_moved_vertices`), the same code every draw tool goes through: a coincident edge welds (no duplicate), a crossing or a touch splits (a T-junction), an edge shrunk to the merge distance goes and its ends merge (no zero-length edge), the tolerance clusters settle in `Sketch::repair`, and the regions are re-detected. A ring that would cross itself is split at the crossing into two regions, so no region is ever a bow-tie. Vertices keep their ids, so a region that only changed shape keeps its id (and the UI keeps it selected); a region whose every vertex moved has its anchor mapped too, so it keeps its id through a split.
- **Off the plane, a whole shape** (the map takes the plane elsewhere and the selection is detachable: no point selected on its own, and every edge left behind that touches a moving vertex meets it on the ring of a region that moves whole) goes to the open context's sketch on the plane it lands on, an existing one or a new one (the same rule `Draw` uses to find a plane's sketch). The carried edges leave the source sketch except where they bound a region that stays, so a neighbour that shared an edge keeps it; a region moves whole when all its ring edges are carried, so an island in a carried hole goes along.
- **A point or edge moved straight off its plane** projects to no move at all: refused, typed (`LeavesSketchPlane`: "select the whole shape to lift it off"). A map that moves nothing (a turn by 0 or 360 degrees) is refused as `NothingToMove`, so it never makes an empty undo step.
- **Copies** (`copy`, with `count` for arrays: copy k mapped by the map applied k times) leave the source alone and draw the carried edges mapped, each copy into the plane it lands on by the same two rules. A copy is independent geometry and welds onto what it touches like a stroke drawn there.
- Refusals are typed and leave the document as it was: a vertex or edge not in the sketch (`NoSuchSketchPoint`, `NoSuchSketchEdge`), a consumed or missing region, a sketch outside the open group (`OutsideContext`), a bad map (`BadTransform`, `Singular`), a point beyond 10 km, a count of 0 or a count without `copy` (`BadCount`).
- Outcomes: `SketchMoved { sketch, regions }` (the sketch the geometry is in now and the live (sketch, region) pairs its edges bound) and `SketchCopied { regions }`.

The tools (`src/tools/xform.ts`) gather sketch entities by sketch into `SketchPart`s alongside the existing subjects, preview the stretched edges with the kernel's projection rule (`sketchPreviews`, `sketchSegments`), and keep the selection on what moved (`movedSelection`: points and edges where they went, regions kept in the plane or replaced by the regions they became on the new plane). With nothing selected, Move, Rotate and Scale pick up the region, sketch edge or sketch point under the cursor. Rotate puts the protractor on the sketch's plane unless an arrow key or Shift chose another, so the default turn keeps a shape in its plane. Push/Pull's next target follows a region lifted onto another plane.

## What the property tests found (and the two kernel fixes)

The hostile generator's 100,000-case runs and the fuzzer found these, none of them in the move logic itself; each was a gap the moves made reachable, and each is fixed in the sketch tier, never by loosening a tolerance:

- **The sketch and the solid measured the same tolerance in different frames.** The sketch merged at POINT_MERGE in its own 2D frame; a region pushed into a solid is measured again in world coordinates with the same POINT_MERGE. An edge 1.0000000000015703e-5 m long in the sketch came out 9.999999999982115e-6 m in the world (zero-length to the solid), and corners that cleared another edge by a rounding error over POINT_MERGE became a vertex on a face (seeds 2026102201, 2026102202, 2026102203). The sketch now makes every decision at `SKETCH_MERGE`, POINT_MERGE plus the 1 nm noise floor (`crates/kernel/src/tolerance.rs`), so whatever it keeps clears the solid tier's tests; the section slice's weld, which must merge everything the sketch merges, follows it.
- **A crossing at infinity.** Two nearly parallel edges the robust predicate saw crossing had a cross product that rounded to zero, and the crossing vertex was put at minus infinity (seed 2026102213, after 19,197 cases; the snapshot refused the sketch, the sketch check did not look). Crossings are now placed by the robust orientations of one edge's ends against the other, finite and on the edge at any angle, and `Sketch::check` refuses a vertex that is not finite or lies beyond 10 km.

- **Areas measured with coordinates a long way out.** The op-sequence fuzzer then moved regions offset 5 km into the open and turned copies of them by 1e-7 degrees (seeds 20268102 to 20268105, 20268201, 20268207, 20268208 and CI's 20261011, 20268107, 20268106): the triangulator's check of a region's triangles summed products of absolute coordinates and lost more than the 1e-9 it allows on correct triangles of a sliver 10 km long and of a region 6 km out, and a section cap 7 km out measured its area 3e-7 short. The check now sums robust triangle areas (`sawhorse_tessellate::area_deviation`, every term accurate to its own size), and ring areas are summed about the ring's first point (`signed_area`, and the cut checker's vector areas), so rounding scales with the shape, not its distance from the origin; and the triangulator calls a polygon "near zero area" against its own extent, not its largest coordinate (a 17 micrometre region 5 km out, 3e-10 m2, had been refused; seed 20268405 and CI's 20261011 again). The same values for everything near the origin: no golden changed.

The sequences are kept in `crates/kernel/tests/sketch_move_finds/` (the property tests') and `crates/kernel/tests/repro/2026-10-19-*` (the fuzzer's) and replayed region by region (`sketch_move.rs`); their seeds stay in `sketch_move_props.proptest-regressions`. No golden changed its hash.

## Consequences

- Every rule above has a kernel test (`crates/kernel/tests/sketch_move.rs`), a hostile property test (`crates/kernel/tests/sketch_move_props.rs`: landings on vertices and edges, turns by 0, 180, 360 and tiny angles, off-plane moves; every region pushed into a solid of its area times the distance; snapshot round trip; undo; rigid maps of a whole sketch congruent) and the op-sequence fuzzer drives the op (`Fuzz::SketchMove`). See `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md` for the runs.
- `.shz` needs nothing new: a moved sketch is a sketch. `.skp` import does not use the op.
- A circle, arc or polygon is still a chain of independent edges ([#3](https://github.com/sawhorse3d/sawhorse/issues/3)): a click on its rim selects one segment or vertex, so moving that moves one point of the curve (within its plane, the neighbours following), not the curve. Moving the region (a click inside) moves it whole. A curve entity is #3's.
- A region whose hole was pushed into a solid (the island under the solid is consumed, not live): lifting the region keeps the hole's loop in the source sketch, under the solid, and draws it at the destination too, where, as every closed loop does (ADR-0001), it forms a face of its own inside the hole.
- Clean room: the behaviour was taken from SketchUp as a user sees it; no SketchUp, OpenSKP or Hew source was read.
