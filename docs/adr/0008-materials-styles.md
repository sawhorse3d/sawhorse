# ADR-0008: Materials, styles and shadows

- Status: Accepted and implemented, 2026-10-12 (lane 10)
- Deciders: the maintainer (the behaviour below was decided before the lane); the lane-10 builder (how the kernel, the worker and the renderer hold it)
- Source: the lane-10 brief; ADR-0005 ("per-instance materials ... not yet"); ADR-0006 ("material is the part's own ... with a per-part override")

## Context

Until lane 10 a material was a name and an sRGB colour (plus the file name of a texture nobody drew), painted on a whole solid by `SetMaterial`. Woodworkers paint by the board: oak sides, a birch ply back, a red top, one face of a drawer front a different colour. They expect SketchUp's habits: paint a face inside an open group, paint a closed group or component to colour what inside it has no paint of its own, Alt+click to pick a material up, textures that follow the wood and can be nudged and turned, styles (hidden line, X-ray) for drawings, and a sun for a sense of depth. The cut list wants the species, and the render service wants to know what the surfaces are.

## Decision

**Behaviour (decided before the lane):**

- A material is a name, a colour, an optional texture (an image with a real-world size, a rotation and an offset) and an optional stock link (lumber or sheet goods, and the species in words). The cut list reads the species from it when the part names no stock of its own.
- Paint goes on faces (inside the open context) or on solids, groups and component instances (their default; faces with a material of their own keep it). Back faces show the default back-face colour. Alt+click samples.
- The library: a built-in starter set of our own (12 woods and 8 sheet goods with procedural textures, 16 flat colours, glass and metal), user materials saved in `.shz` with their images, an image imported as a material, colour / size / rotation edited live.
- Texture positioning v1: scale, rotation and offset per face with on-screen handles; skew and projection later.
- Styles: edges (on, profiles, extensions, endpoints; jitter never), faces (shaded with textures, shaded, monochrome, hidden line, wireframe, X-ray), background / sky / ground colours, section cuts (kept for the section planes of a later lane), six presets; saved per document and in scenes.
- Shadows: a sun by date, time and latitude (no geolocation), on / off, light and dark, on the ground and on faces; a GPU shadow map.
- Render passes carry material hints. `.skp` import maps materials, textures and positions; `.shz` stores everything.

**How it is built (`crates/kernel/src/looks.rs`):**

- **Kernel state.** `Material` gains `size`, `rotation`, `offset` and `stock`. A solid carries `paint`: per persistent face id, a material and a `UvXform` (scale, rotation, offset); only faces with something said are listed. Groups and instances carry a `material` (their default); a nested node's lives in its definition member, a top-level instance's is its own (so two copies of a component can be painted differently, which ADR-0005 left for later). The model holds texture images (`TextureImage`: PNG or JPEG bytes behind an `Arc`, their pixel size read from the header, a FNV-1a digest), a `Style` and a `Shadows`. Scenes may carry a style and shadows; activating such a scene brings them back.
- **What a face shows** (`Model::face_material`): its own material, else its solid's, else the nearest enclosing group or instance that has one. The view mesh carries the effective material per part, its colour, whether it is the face's own, the face's positioning, texture coordinates per vertex and the two face normals at each edge (for profiles).
- **Paint follows edits.** `assign_face_ids` now reports the lineage of faces that lost a duplicate id (the second piece of a split), and the solid copies the paint across; strokes and imprints on a painted face's plane pass its paint to the faces they make; paint of faces that disappear goes with them; push/pull walls are new faces and show the default. Component templates carry the paint like the material (captured, re-derived, compared by the validator).
- **Texture coordinates** come from the face's stored plane frame (which moves with the solid, its copies and component placements, so a texture stays on the wood), turned a quarter so the image's width runs along the face's longer side (grain along the board), then the material's and the face's offset, rotation and size. The same function feeds the view, the exporters and the handles' live preview. `.skp` mappings arrive as world affine maps and are fitted to that form (rotation and per-axis scale kept, skew dropped).
- **Ops.** `DefineMaterial` (extended, all new fields optional), `RenameMaterial`, `DeleteMaterial`, `AddTexture` (base64 in JSON; path `assets/<name>.png|.jpg`, type checked against the bytes, at most 16 MB and 512 images), `Paint` (faces, or items), `SetFaceUv`, `SetStyle`, `SetShadows`. Sampling is a query (`Model::sample`). Painting a locked item is refused. Applying a library material is a batch (`AddTexture`, `DefineMaterial`, `Paint`): one undo step.
- **Hashing.** Everything new is hashed only when present (`Model::has_looks`), so every older golden keeps its hash; a plain colour material painted with `Paint` on a solid is the same state as `SetMaterial`. New golden: `painted.json` (WASM == native).
- **The cut list** names a part by the material covering most of its first solid's faces (face paint, else the solid's, else what is around it), which is the old behaviour when nothing is painted by face; a stock link turns it into the species and decides lumber or sheet goods unless the part says otherwise.
- **The library** (`crates/export/src/library.rs`) draws its textures itself: value noise on a periodic integer lattice (every image tiles), integer hashing and `+ - * /` only, written by our own PNG writer (`crates/export/src/png.rs`, zlib through `flate2`, already in the tree through `zip`), so the bytes are identical natively and in WASM. No image is downloaded or copied from anywhere.
- **Across the boundary.** The worker sends each texture image to the UI once, by digest, as a transferred buffer; the view mesh's texture coordinates and edge normals are transferred typed arrays like the rest. The handles' live preview asks the worker for one face's coordinates (`face_uvs`) and patches the vertex buffer.
- **Rendering (`src/viewport/engine.ts`, `src/viewport/looks.ts`).** Unlit by default, exactly as before (the theme's three tones, material colours shaded the same way), so the corpus images and every pixel test are unchanged; textured faces are drawn by one overlay mesh per image over the faces (same positions and depth offset, `LessEqual`), sRGB, repeat, mipmaps and the renderer's best anisotropy. Hidden line paints faces in the background colour; wireframe hides them and shows every edge; X-ray draws them see-through with every edge. Profiles are found from the edge normals and the camera each frame the camera moves (fat lines), extensions lengthen every edge by a few pixels, endpoints draw short heavy ticks. The sky and ground are a full-screen backdrop whose horizon follows the camera's pitch. With shadows on, the faces switch to a lit material under a directional sun (PCF soft shadow map, 2048) and an ambient light set by the light and dark sliders, and a shadow-catching ground plane; the sun below the horizon casts nothing.
- **Exports.** GLB: one primitive per material a solid shows, images embedded with a repeating sampler and `TEXCOORD_0` (the `.shz`'s `geometry.glb` leaves the images out: they are `assets/` entries). OBJ: `usemtl` per face where materials differ, `vt` on textured faces, `map_Kd` naming the image file the app saves beside the MTL. SVG view drawings follow the document's edge style.

- **With nesting (ADR-0007).** A material's stock link and the stock library's per-material default answer different questions: the link says what the cut list calls the part (the species) and whether it is sheet goods; the default says which sheet or board the nesting cuts it from, keyed by the name the cut list reads. Renaming a material read by its own name moves its default along (one undo step); a material read by its species leaves the default on the species.

## Consequences

- Every older document, golden and corpus file reads, hashes and renders as before; the new behaviour appears only when it is used.
- Paint is one undo step per click (a batch for a selection or a library material); a slider in the Styles pane previews live and commits once on release.
- Two copies of a component can show different paint (per-instance defaults), while paint on faces inside a component is the definition's.
- Not yet: skewed and projected texture positions (dropped on import with a note), painting back faces, translucent sorting beyond three.js's per-object order, section planes (the style keeps the flag), and the render service using `material_hints` beyond the sentence the app adds to the prompt.
